# MaenPDF Brand Policy

MaenPDF is free and open-source software. The source code is licensed under GPL-3.0-or-later as stated in `LICENSE`.

The **MaenPDF** name and official logo identify the upstream project and its trusted releases. This brand policy is separate from the source-code license.

## Community contributions

Contributors may submit improvements under their own real name or organization name. Accepted contributors may be credited in the application About page, release notes, Git history, and project documentation without changing the MaenPDF product name.

## Builds using the MaenPDF name

A build may use the exact MaenPDF name and official logo when it is an official upstream release or when it clearly identifies itself as a community build derived from MaenPDF and does not misrepresent who published or signed it.

Modified forks that are distributed as independent products must not imply that they are official upstream releases. If a fork materially changes security, privacy, update, or signing behavior, it should use a distinct product identity or clearly label itself as an unofficial MaenPDF-based build.

## Security and signing

Official signing keys, store credentials, and release secrets are never part of the source repository. A third party must not claim that its binaries are signed or endorsed by the MaenPDF project unless that statement is true.

This policy is intended to protect users from confusing or malicious binaries while keeping the code genuinely open for study, improvement, and redistribution under the GPL.
